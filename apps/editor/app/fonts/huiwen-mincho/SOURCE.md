being represented as an OFL release.

## Local webfont conversion

The complete original TrueType font was converted with fontTools 4.66.1 and
Brotli 1.2.0 using `TTFont`, `font.flavor = 'woff2'`, and `font.save(...)`.
The standard WOFF2 transformations and Brotli quality 11 were used. No glyph
subsetting, redrawing, or AI generation was performed. Tooling was installed
only in the task's scratch directory, without changing project dependencies.
